Veeraswamy: A Rare Achiever

A man from North India was running the Dreamland film distribution office in Bengaluru. Veeraswamy joined the organisation as an ordinary employee and, through sheer hard work and determination, rose to great heights. When circumstances forced the owner to shut down the film distribution company, the North Indian proprietor entrusted the organisation to Veeraswamy and left. Veeraswamy later renamed the company Eshwari Pictures and began his journey in film distribution.

Driven by his love for cinema, self-confidence, and foresight, Veeraswamy never looked back. He went on to make a name for himself in film production and distribution. His contribution to the growth of the Kannada film industry has been immense. Films such as Kulagaurava, Nagarahavu, Naa Ninna Mareyalare, and Naari Swargakke Daari, along with co-productions such as Bhootayyana Maga Ayyu, Hemavathi, Bhoolokadalli Yamaraja, and Naarada Vijaya, were produced under the banner of his company.

Veeraswamy, who brought grandeur and scale to Kannada cinema, later produced films such as Chakravyuha, Premaloka, Ranadheera, and Shanti Kranti for his son. He also produced films in Hindi and Tamil. Following the path laid by his father, Ravichandran went on to create another remarkable success story. Several artists and technicians were introduced to the film industry through their production house.

At the centenary celebration of Bhootayyana Maga Ayyu (1974): Director Siddalingaiah, producers Varadappa and Veeraswamy, Dr. Rajkumar, the legendary actor's mother Lakshmamma, and Parvathamma Rajkumar.
