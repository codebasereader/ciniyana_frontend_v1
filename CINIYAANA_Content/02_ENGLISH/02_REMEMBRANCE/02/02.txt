Sundar Krishna Aras: The Actor with a Resonant Voice

Sundar Krishna Aras was an actor known for his deep, resonant voice in the Kannada film industry. His native place was Uttavalli, near Chamarajanagar. After completing his high school education in Yelandur, he earned a B.Com. degree from Marimallappa College in Mysuru. While pursuing his studies, he acted in plays, which sparked his interest in acting and direction. After completing his education, he worked as a professional theatre artist with several drama companies for some time.

He entered the film industry as an assistant director. In his early days, he gained valuable experience working as an assistant director on Puttanna Kanagal's films, including Kappu Bilupu, Mallammana Pavada, Gejje Pooje, Karulina Kare, Sharapanjara, and Sakshatkara. During this period, he was also recognised as a dubbing artist. His voice was a perfect fit for Hindi actor Amrish Puri in Girish Karnad's Kaadu. The role of Permadi in Karnad's Ondanondu Kaladalli proved to be a turning point in Aras's acting career. He went on to act in more than 150 films, predominantly in villainous and supporting roles.

Chakra, Sangya Balya, Super Nova 459, and the Tulu film Sangama Sakshi were among the films he directed. He also acted in the Telugu version of Anta titled Antam Kaadidi Aarambham, as well as Kamal Haasan's Tamil film Punnagai Mannan.

Renowned Indian film editor Suresh Aras was Sundar Krishna Aras's cousin. His elder son, Nagendra Aras, has made a name for himself in the Kannada film industry as an editor and film director.

Sundar Krishna Aras passed away on November 10, 1993, at the age of 52.
