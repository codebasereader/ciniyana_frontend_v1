Poet K. S. Narasimhaswamy Wrote Songs for Specific Situations

Whenever one thinks of Nagarabharana’s Mysore Mallige, poet K. S. Narasimhaswamy naturally comes to mind. But much earlier, in 1969, he had written songs for the film Anireekshita. The film was produced and directed by Nagesh Babu.

It is said that KSN came to Madras and wrote songs specifically to suit the situations in the film. (Nagesh Babu was the elder brother of still photographer Pragathi Ashwathanarayana.)

Arrangements were made for Narasimhaswamy to stay at the old Woodlands Hotel in Madras. During his four-day stay in the city, music composer Vijaya Bhaskar and singer P. B. Srinivas were with him.

For different situations in the film, Vijaya Bhaskar composed the tunes, and KSN wrote the songs “Bandaramma Bandaru,” “Ondu Balliyalondu Hoo,” and “Maitumbida Ee Cheluvige…”

The song “Maitumbida Ee Cheluvige…”, sung by L. R. Eswari, became popular at the time.

Narasimhaswamy’s poems had been used in other films even before this. However, it is believed that Anireekshita was the first film for which he specifically wrote songs to suit the situations in the screenplay.

According to Ashwathanarayana’s recollection, KSN was paid a remuneration of ₹150 for each song.
