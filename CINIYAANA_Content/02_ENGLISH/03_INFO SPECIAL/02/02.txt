Rajkumar–Jayanthi Pair: 36 Films

Jayanthi holds the distinction of being the leading lady who acted opposite Dr. Rajkumar in the highest number of films. Jayanthi acted in 39 films with Rajkumar, and in 36 of them, she was paired opposite him.

Leelavathi acted in 45 films with Rajkumar, but was paired with him in 29 films. Bharathi, meanwhile, holds the unique distinction of having been paired opposite Rajkumar in all 26 films in which she acted with him.

(Information courtesy: Dr. K. Puttaswamy’s book ‘Cinemayana’)
