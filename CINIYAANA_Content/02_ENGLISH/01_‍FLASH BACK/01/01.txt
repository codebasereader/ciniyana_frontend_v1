‘Bhootayyana Maga Ayyu’ Centenary Celebration

A moment from the centenary celebration of Bhootayyana Maga Ayyu (1974), directed by Siddalingaiah, held at Hotel Woodlands in Bengaluru. Actor Dr. Rajkumar, writer Gorur Ramaswamy Iyengar, and screenwriter Hunsur Krishnamurthy are seen here.

Based on Gorur Ramaswamy Iyengar’s short story Vayyari, Bhootayyana Maga Ayyu is regarded as one of the significant films in Kannada cinema. Two other novels by Gorur, Hemavathi and Urvashi, were also adapted into films under the same titles. Siddalingaiah and Amaradeva directed these films, respectively.
