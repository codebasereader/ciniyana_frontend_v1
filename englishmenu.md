FLASH BACK
Writer and screenwriter Jayanth Kaikini says, “Cinema theatres are temples that lift us above the worldly turmoil and protect us.” This ‘Flashback’ is a nostalgic journey through cherished memories of cinema, featuring movie stills and behind-the-scenes working stills from film shoots!
------------------------
REMEMBRANCE
While actors and actresses captivate audiences with their performances on screen, the technicians who work behind the scenes often remain unsung heroes. This section remembers these veteran technicians and celebrates their remarkable contributions to cinema.
-------------------------
INFO SPECIAL
A collection of surprising and rare information and special features from the world of cinema. These short write-ups about Kannada film actors, actresses, and technicians will satisfy the curiosity of cinema enthusiasts while deepening their love for cinema.
-------------------------
VIDEO
A variety of engaging videos that will entertain and delight viewers. This segment will primarily introduce the unsung technicians of cinema, who often work behind the scenes, accompanied by rare photographs.
--------------------------
POSTER
People often recognize a film through its poster. From the very beginning of a film’s journey to even after its release, the poster serves as its visual identity and calling card. This section presents information about films through attractive and captivating poster designs.
---------------------------
PHOTO-STORY
Photographs tell stories. Cinema-related photographs, in particular, hold countless stories and fascinating details within them. This is an effort to document those moments and tell the stories behind the photographs.
-----------------------------
OFF THE CAMERA
An engaging section that brings to life the wonders, surprises, joys, and challenges behind the camera. While we see the images created in front of the camera on the screen, this unique section takes cinema lovers behind the scenes and tells the untold stories of filmmaking.
-----------------------------
ARTICLE
This is a collection of writings on cinema. It features perspectives, opinions, analyses, and notes on films from various languages. Through these writings, the authors share their views and offer valuable insights to fellow cinema enthusiasts.
-----------------------------
FILM TODAY
A journey through the history of the silver screen, while also tracing the latest milestones and developments in the world of cinema. This segment serves as a bridge to connect directly with the film industry, contemporary cinema artists, and technicians.
